Quantum Coaster
Dare to defy physics and ride the future.

Step into the unknown on Quantum Coaster, a high-velocity journey through space and time. As you launch forward, light bends, stars blur, and gravity twists around you in an electrifying dance of science and speed. This isn’t just a ride — it’s an experience that redefines thrill.

Safety Information
  • Secure all loose items before riding
  • High-speed launch + inversions
  • Guests must remain seated with restraints locked
  • Not recommended for guests with heart, neck, or back issues
  • Expect strong G-forces and sudden movements
  • Pregnant guests are not permitted
Queue Time / Ride Duration
  • Average Queue Time: 45–60 minutes
  • Ride Duration: 2 minutes 20 seconds
Height/Age Requirement
  • Minimum Height: 130 cm
  • Recommended Age: 10+

Neon Lagoon
Peaceful. Hypnotic. Otherworldly.

Where light meets water, and reality starts to shimmer. Neon Lagoon glows with radiant hues that shift with every ripple — a serene yet surreal paradise. Glide through illuminated waters on glowing boats, surrounded by holographic waves and ambient synth melodies.

Safety Information
  • Water-based attraction with stepping platforms and holographic sprays
  • Floors may be slippery — walk carefully
  • Life jackets required for small children
  • No running or pushing
  • Oversized bags should be stored in lockers
  • Safe for all ages with adult supervision
Queue Time / Ride Duration
  • Average Queue Time: 15–30 minutes
  • Experience Duration: 5–7 minutes
Height/Age Requirement
  • Minimum Height: 100 cm (without life jacket)
  • Minimum Height: No minimum if wearing life jacket
  • Recommended Age: All ages

Eclipse Dome
Where darkness reveals the light.

Witness day and night merge in a breathtaking 360° spectacle. Inside the Eclipse Dome, cutting-edge projection tech brings cosmic wonders to life — from solar flares to lunar eclipses — all choreographed with music and motion. It’s not just a show, it’s an awakening of the senses.

Safety Information
  • Features intense strobe lighting and 360° projection effects
  • Not suitable for guests with epilepsy or severe motion sickness
  • Guests must remain seated during the show
  • No food, drinks, or flash photography inside
  • Safe for children under supervision
Queue Time / Ride Duration
  • Average Queue Time: 10–20 minutes
  • Show Duration: 8 minutes
Height/Age Requirement
  • Minimum Height: No height requirement
  • Recommended Age: 5+ (Younger children allowed with guardian)
