Opening Hours

NeoPark operates daily:

Monday – Sunday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Special event days may extend up to 1:00 AM

Directions to NeoPark

NeoPark is located in the heart of Neo District, Cyber Valley, Selangor.


By Car:
  • 25 minutes from Kuala Lumpur
  • Parking available at the Visitor Parking Zone
  • Real-time parking capacity displayed at entrance

By Public Transport:
  • Take MRT/SkyRail Line 2
  • Stop at Cyber Valley Station
  • Walk 5 minutes to the main entrance (signs will guide you)

Shuttle Service:
  • Every 20 minutes
  • From Cyber Valley Station → NeoPark Main Gate
Safety Guidelines

NeoPark is designed with advanced safety systems to ensure a safe and enjoyable visit.


  • General Park Safety
    • Follow staff instructions at all times
    • Keep hydrated during long visits
    • Supervise young children closely
    • No running in wet or high-traffic zones
  • Attraction Safety
    • Respect height and age requirements
    • Loose items should be stored in lockers
    • Follow seatbelt and restraint instructions
    • Visitors with heart, neck, or back issues should avoid high-intensity rides
    • Pregnant guests should avoid thrill attractions
  • Weather & Environmental Safety
    • Some outdoor attractions may pause during storms
    • Misting stations available during hot weather
    • Shaded resting zones located throughout the park
Accessibility Services

NeoPark is designed to be inclusive and accessible to all guests.


  • Wheelchair Accessibility
    • Wheelchair-friendly pathways across the park
    • Elevators available at multi-level attractions
    • Accessible entrances for Eclipse Dome and several shows
  • Special Assistance
    • Priority boarding for guests with disabilities
    • Assistance cards available at Guest Services
    • Quiet rooms available for sensory-sensitive guests
  • Rental Services
    • Wheelchair rentals
    • Stroller rentals
    • Electric mobility vehicles (limited units)
